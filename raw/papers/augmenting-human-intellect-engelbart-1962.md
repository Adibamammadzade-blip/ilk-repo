# Augmenting Human Intellect: A Conceptual Framework

**Author:** Douglas C. Engelbart  
**Published:** Stanford Research Institute (SRI) Summary Report AFOSR-3223, October 1962  
**Context:** Foundational framework for computer-supported cooperative work, interactive computing, and intellectual augmentation.

---

## I. Introduction & General Statement of the Problem

By "augmenting human intellect" we mean increasing the capability of a man to approach a complex problem situation, to gain comprehension to suit his particular needs, and to derive solutions to problems. 

Increased capability in this respect is taken to mean a mixture of the following: 
- More-rapid comprehension; 
- Better comprehension; 
- The possibility of gaining a useful degree of comprehension in a situation that previously was too complex; 
- Spicier, more imaginative solutions; 
- Solutions that can be found in a shorter time; 
- Better solutions in situations where the time limit is critical.

We do not speak of isolated clever tricks that help in particular situations, but of a way of life in an integrated domain where radishes, carrots, ideas, symbols, and artifacts exist together in a system.

---

## II. The H-LAM/T System (Human using Language, Artifacts, Methodology, and Training)

We view the augmented human as a system consisting of a human being collaborating with four primary sub-elements:

1. **Language:** The way in which the individual parcels out the picture of his world into the concepts that his mind uses to model that world, and the symbols that he attaches to those concepts and uses in conscious thought processes.
2. **Artifacts:** Physical objects designed to provide for human comfort, for the manipulation of things or materials, and for the manipulation of symbols (e.g., pen, paper, computer displays, storage repositories).
3. **Methodology:** The methods, procedures, and strategies with which an individual organizes his goal-directed problem-solving activity.
4. **Training:** The conditioning and skill development that the human receives to operate effectively within the Language, Artifact, and Methodology triad.

We refer to this composite system as the **H-LAM/T** system (**H**uman using **L**anguage, **A**rtifacts, **M**ethodology, and **T**raining).

When an individual's capabilities are augmented, it is accomplished by introducing improvements to one or more of these four components, and re-optimizing the whole system.

---

## III. Symbol Manipulation & Externalized Structure

Man's capability to solve complex problems relies entirely on his capacity to manipulate symbols externally. Internal memory is severely bounded in working capacity (the magic number $7 \pm 2$). By transcribing concepts into external visual artifacts (external storage), the thinker can construct conceptual pyramids of arbitrary depth.

In a mechanized external symbol-manipulation environment, human thought is freed from serial linear constraints. Concepts can be arranged in multidimensional hierarchies, interconnected with flexible trails, and viewed from multiple semantic perspectives simultaneously.

---

## IV. Bootstrapping & Intellectual Symbiosis

The process of developing tools to augment human intelligence is inherently **recursive** (or *bootstrapping*). By using the best existing augmentation tools to design the next generation of augmentation tools, the rate of intellectual capability expansion accelerates compounding interest.

The relationship between the human thinker and the external symbol processor is not master-and-slave, nor automated replacement, but **intellectual symbiosis**: the machine handles structured information retrieval, formal rule execution, and multi-variable consistency, allowing the human to focus on conceptual synthesis, intuitive leaps, and goal determination.
