# Douglas Engelbart

## Overview
**Dr. Douglas C. Engelbart** (1925–2013) was an American engineer, inventor, and computing pioneer who founded the Augmentation Research Center (ARC) at Stanford Research Institute (SRI). He is renowned for presenting **"The Mother of All Demos"** in 1968, which introduced the computer mouse, interactive hypertext, video conferencing, collaborative real-time text editing, and graphical user interfaces.

## Major Inventions & Contributions
- **The Computer Mouse (1964):** Patented the X-Y position indicator for a display system.
- **NLS (oN-Line System):** The world's first working implementation of collaborative hypertext and screen-based symbol manipulation.
- **Augmenting Human Intellect Framework (1962):** Formalized the [[h-lam-t-system|H-LAM/T System]] for expanding human problem-solving capacity.
- **Bootstrapping Concept:** The methodology of using cognitive tools to design better cognitive tools, generating compounding intellectual leverage.

## Role in the LLM Wiki Ecosystem
- Engelbart provided the theoretical paradigm for **Human-AI Symbiosis**: computers manipulate, index, and organize externalized symbols while humans provide high-level intuition and direction.
