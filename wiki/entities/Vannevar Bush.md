# Vannevar Bush

**Vannevar Bush** (1890–1974) was an American engineer, inventor, and science administrator who headed the U.S. Office of Scientific Research and Development (OSRD) during World War II.

## Key Contributions
- Author of the 1945 essay *"As We May Think"*, published in *The Atlantic Monthly*.
- Conceived the theoretical [[Memex]] device, introducing the concepts of associative indexing, trails of discovery, and personal information workstations.
- Widely recognized as a foundational figure in the history of hypertext, the World Wide Web, and personal knowledge management.
