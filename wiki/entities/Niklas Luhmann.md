# Niklas Luhmann

**Niklas Luhmann** (1927–1998) was a prominent German sociologist and systems theorist known for his prolific academic output (over 70 books and 400 scholarly articles).

## Note-Taking System
Luhmann attributed his extraordinary productivity to his physical slip-box system, the [[Zettelkasten Method]]. He treated his slip-box not as a static repository of quotes, but as an interactive dialogue partner capable of generating unexpected conceptual insights.

## Influence on Modern Knowledge Management
Luhmann's principles inspired modern digital toolsets (Obsidian, Roam, Logseq) and directly prefigure the [[LLM Wiki Pattern]], where the knowledge base acts as a persistent, compounding conversational collaborator.
