# Memex

The **Memex** (a portmanteau of "memory" and "index") is a theoretical electro-mechanical personal knowledge workstation described by [[Vannevar Bush]] in his seminal 1945 essay *"As We May Think"*.

## Key Principles
- **Associative Trails**: Rather than rigid alphabetical or numerical hierarchies, information is linked associatively, mirroring the human brain's web of connections.
- **Compounding Personal Repository**: A personal archive of books, records, and communications that grows alongside the user over a lifetime.
- **Direct Interaction**: Ability to annotate, cross-reference, and build customized trails of research.

## Connection to [[LLM Wiki Pattern]]
Bush's primary bottleneck in 1945 was the mechanical labor of index maintenance. The [[LLM Wiki Pattern]] solves this by placing an autonomous AI agent in charge of maintaining and updating associative trails, creating a digital Memex for the modern era.
