# Zettelkasten Method

The **Zettelkasten** (German for "slip-box") is a knowledge management and note-taking methodology developed by sociologist [[Niklas Luhmann]].

## Core Principles
1. **Atomicity**: Each note contains exactly one discrete idea or argument.
2. **Associative Density**: Notes are heavily interlinked using explicit IDs or [[Wikilinks]] rather than isolated in hierarchical folders.
3. **Note Taxonomies**:
   - **Fleeting Notes**: Quick temporary thoughts.
   - **Literature Notes**: Structured summaries of raw sources.
   - **Permanent Notes**: Self-contained, evergreen conceptual building blocks.
4. **Emergence**: Structure emerges organically from bottom-up connections rather than top-down taxonomy.

## Application in [[ilk-repo]]
The [[LLM Wiki Pattern]] directly implements Zettelkasten philosophy:
- `wiki/sources/` maps to **Literature Notes**.
- `wiki/concepts/` and `wiki/entities/` map to **Permanent Notes**.
- `wiki/synthesis/` maps to **Structure Notes** (Thematic overviews).
