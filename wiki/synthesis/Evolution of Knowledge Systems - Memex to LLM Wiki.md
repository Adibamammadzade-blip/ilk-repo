# Evolution of Knowledge Systems: From Memex to LLM Wiki

An overarching comparative synthesis exploring the 80-year evolution of personal knowledge tools, culminating in persistent AI-compiled knowledge graphs.

---

## 1. Historical Timeline & Paradigm Shifts

```text
1945: Memex (Vannevar Bush)
  └─ Concept of associative trails & personal information storage

1960s-1980s: Zettelkasten (Niklas Luhmann)
  └─ Bottom-up emergence, atomic notes, bidirectional linking

2020s: Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
  └─ Vector embedding similarity, ephemeral per-query chunk retrieval

2026+: The LLM Wiki Pattern
  └─ Autonomous LLM maintenance, persistent compounding markdown, compiled graph
```

## 3. The Resolution of the Maintenance Bottleneck

For decades, personal knowledge bases suffered from a fatal economic reality: **the maintenance burden scaled faster than the value of the knowledge base**.
- Human note-takers eventually abandoned their wikis due to the immense effort required to update cross-references, summarize literature, and reorganize indexes.
- With the [[LLM Wiki Pattern]], the cost of maintenance drops to zero. The human directs exploration, asks questions, and sources raw documents, while the AI performs the continuous bookkeeping, linting, and synthesis.
